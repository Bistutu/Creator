## English

Creator 0.0.2 updates window closing and reopening for generated website apps on macOS.

- Press `⌘X` or `⌘W`, or click the red close button, to close the current window. The app stays running after the last window closes. Press `⌘Q` to quit. Use **Edit → Cut** to cut text.
- Reopen from the Dock or from Creator to resume the same page with its unsaved input.
- To update an app you already created, restart Creator and open that app from the library. If the app was still running, quit it with `⌘Q` and open it again to load the updated launcher.

## 中文

Creator 0.0.2 更新了 macOS 独立网站应用关闭和重开窗口的方式。

- 按 `⌘X`、`⌘W` 或点击红色按钮关闭当前窗口。最后一个窗口关闭后，应用继续运行。按 `⌘Q` 退出应用，剪切可通过「编辑 → 剪切」完成。
- 从程序坞或 Creator 重开应用时，会回到原网页并保留未保存的输入。
- 已创建的应用可以在重启 Creator 后从应用库打开并更新。仍在运行的应用需要按一次 `⌘Q`，再次打开时会使用新的启动器。
